My troubleshooting approach

A structured process, guided by evidence.

I adjust the order of checks to match the impact, available workaround and evidence collected.

  1. 01

    I understand the user’s report and the business impact.

  2. 02

    I confirm scope, urgency and whether a workaround exists.

  3. 03

    I gather evidence before making changes.

  4. 04

    I test the simplest likely causes first.

  5. 05

    I let each result shape the next check.

  6. 06

    I resolve or escalate with useful context.

  7. 07

    I verify service and ask the user to confirm.

  8. 08

    I document the outcome and preventive action.

“My goal is not only to fix the technology. I want to reduce downtime, restore the user’s ability to work and leave a clear record for the next person who sees the issue.”
